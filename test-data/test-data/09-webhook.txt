=== 09. Webhook — "Your consumer endpoint + events" ===

This method registers YOUR consumer endpoint and sends HMAC-signed sample events to it.
Fill these into the Webhook form:

  Consumer endpoint:  https://httpbin.org/post
  Secret:             whsec_demo_123

  Sample event payloads:
    {"event":"order.created","id":"ord_1001","amount":4200,"currency":"USD"}
    {"event":"order.refunded","id":"ord_1001","amount":4200,"currency":"USD"}

Deliveries are signed with the secret; httpbin.org/post echoes them back so you can
verify the signature header and body in real time.
