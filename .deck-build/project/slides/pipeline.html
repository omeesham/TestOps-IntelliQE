The gate sits between 03 and 04

An endpoint whose requirement is still draft or rejected never reaches the planner, and the person is told which ones were held back.

Nothing old was moved

An endpoint with no captured requirement passes straight through, so every existing spec-first workflow behaves exactly as it did before.

Steps 2, 3 and 6 are the new ones. The decision not to make the gate mandatory is what let all of this ship without a migration: a team that never opens the Requirements tab sees no change at all.
