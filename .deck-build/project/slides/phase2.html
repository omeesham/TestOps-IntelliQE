Edit in place

A reviewer can rewrite the purpose, the inputs, the expected responses and the rules before anything is planned.

Editing undoes approval

Saving a change returns the requirement to draft, so an approval can never outlive the text it was given for.

Triage at speed

Filter chips by state with live counts, and approve or reject from the card without opening it.

The “editing returns it to draft” rule came out of thinking about what an approval actually certifies. It certifies a specific piece of text. Let the text change underneath and the approval becomes a signature on a blank page.
